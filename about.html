Who Is notyet?

notyet is my developer name. I like building story-driven projects, experimenting with tone, and documenting process as I go.

I believe in making things that feel honest, even when they're strange.

What This Site Is For

This site is my personal space first. I share who I am, what I'm building, and what I'm learning through each project.

It's also a love letter to the old web — a place where every site felt like someone's room.

Main Project

Currently making Sophie Stops the Sun — a game about a girl who doesn't want to move, a deal with the sun, and a bug civilization growing in the grass.
