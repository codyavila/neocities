Overview

Sophie Stops the Sun is a narrative game about fear of change, saying goodbye, and finding hope in uncertain places.

  • Genre: Story-driven adventure
  • Tone: Tender, strange, and bittersweet
  • Inspirations: Undertale, EarthBound, Omori, James and the Giant Peach

The Premise

Sophie hides from tomorrow. Beneath her backyard, she finds a tiny civilization of bugs who worship the shade and dread the sunrise. To stay, she must stop the sun — but nothing stays still forever.
