Fragment 01 — The Deal

She said: "If I win, tomorrow doesn't come."
The sun said nothing. It just started counting.

Fragment 02 — The Loud Hour

The bugs call sunrise "the loud hour." They say the light makes shadows brittle and names fall off things. Nobody goes outside during the loud hour. Nobody has for a long, long time.

Fragment 03 — Tiny Architecture

Tunnels. Towers. Whole neighborhoods made of bottle caps and thread. At the top of the tallest tower was a sign: "DO NOT LOOK EAST." Sophie looked east anyway. There was nothing there yet.

Fragment 04 — The Elder's Warning

"The sun doesn't want to hurt you," the eldest bug said. "It just doesn't know how to be gentle." He paused. "Neither does tomorrow."

Fragment 05 — Tomorrow at the Fence

Tomorrow stood at the fence line all night, waiting to be invited in. Sophie pretended not to see it. The bugs pretended harder.

Fragment 06 — Sophie's Notebook

Found scrawled on the last page, in crayon: "New school. New house. New everything. I want none of it. If I stay very still, maybe today won't end."

Fragment 07 — Beautiful in the Shade

They built something beautiful down here. All of it in the shade, all of it afraid, and all of it still growing. Sophie sat in the middle of it and thought: maybe I can too.
