who is notyet?

Great question. I'm notyet. That's not my real name obviously but we're on the internet so let's just roll with it.

I make things. Mostly a game right now. Sometimes I make websites instead of working on the game because I'm avoiding my responsibilities. I like stories, bugs (the insect kind), and staying up way too late.

I'm probably fine.

I don't know what I'm doing but I'm doing it with confidence
