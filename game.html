the premise

Sophie lives at the edge of a neighborhood that's about to change. Her family is moving. She doesn't want to go.

One evening she makes a deal with the sun: if it never sets, tomorrow never comes, and she never has to leave. The sun agrees — but the world doesn't stop just because the sky won't turn.

if the sun never sets, tomorrow never comes

the bugs

In the tall grass behind Sophie's house, a civilization of bugs has been growing. They have their own culture, their own struggles, and their own relationship with the endless daylight Sophie created.

The bugs didn't ask for a world without night. Some of them are thriving. Some of them are not.

what the game is about

It's about the gap between holding on and letting go. About how the things we do to protect ourselves ripple outward. About saying goodbye to a place and realizing the place was never really yours.

"saying goodbye doesn't have to mean losing everything"
