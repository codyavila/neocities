why neocities?

first post

I wanted a place that felt like mine. Not a profile on someone else's platform. Not a feed that disappears. Something I could build by hand and come back to.

Neocities reminded me of the old web — when every site felt like walking into someone's room. You could tell what they cared about by the colors they picked and the things they linked to.

That's what I want this to be. A room. My room. You're welcome to look around.
