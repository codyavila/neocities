Current Project

I'm building Sophie Stops the Sun. Sophie is moving tomorrow — new school, new house, new everything. She wants none of it. So she makes a deal with the sun: a game of hide and seek. If she hides long enough, maybe tomorrow just... forgets to show up.

While she's been hiding, a whole civilization of bugs has grown in the grass around her. They're afraid of the sun too. And somewhere in their world, Sophie starts to wonder if saying goodbye doesn't have to mean losing everything.
