building in the open, breaking things on purpose

entry #3 — figuring out the bugs

date: tbd

The bug civilization is the part I keep coming back to. How do you build a culture that feels real when your characters are three millimeters tall?

I've been sketching out social structures — the way they organize around light sources now that Sophie stopped the sun. Some bugs worship the eternal day. Others are building shelters to simulate night.

"they didn't choose this world. but they're making something of it."

entry #2 — tone and feeling

date: tbd

Figured out the tone I want: it should feel like the last day of summer. That specific ache when you know something good is ending and you can't do anything about it.

The game needs to be warm even when it's sad. Sunlight through curtains, not rain on windows.

entry #1 — the beginning

date: tbd

Started working on Sophie Stops the Sun. The core idea: a girl makes a deal with the sun to stop it from setting, because if tomorrow comes, she has to move away. The sun agrees.

Now I need to figure out what happens next. What does a world with no night look like? What grows in endless light?

  • Core concept locked in
  • Bug civilization idea emerging
  • Need to decide on engine
